How does Betadine work?

Every step in the platform is designed to make your study journey easier and clearer:

Choose your class and subject

First, you choose your grade level (first, second, or third year of secondary school), and then you choose the subject you want to study.

Check out the teachers and courses

You will find a list of the best teachers for each subject, and courses divided by topic and review.

Enroll in the course

Found a course you like? Click "Enroll Now" and start learning immediately! You can access it anytime from your mobile phone or laptop.

Study and practice.

Watch videos, solve exams, earn points, and return to the content whenever you like.

Ask, participate, and compete.

Join discussion groups, ask about anything unclear, share your ideas, and see where you stand among your peers.
